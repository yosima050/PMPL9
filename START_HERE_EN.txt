STUDENT PACKAGE | MEETING 09 | LARAVEL DUSK
============================================
Start with jobsheets/Worksheet_P09_Laravel_Beginner_EN.docx.

Complete first-time setup (PHP, Composer, Chrome, Laravel/Dusk) BEFORE the 120-minute lab session.

1. Extract the ZIP into a simple local folder.
2. Open Terminal/PowerShell in the extracted folder containing installer/ and project_files/.
3. Run: php installer/cek_prasyarat.php
4. One-time setup: php installer/pasang_proyek.php
5. In Terminal A: cd sib-qa-lab, then php artisan serve --host=127.0.0.1 --port=8000
6. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 in a browser.
7. In Terminal B (inside sib-qa-lab): php artisan dusk --filter=test_contoh_halaman_produk
8. Follow Worksheet 09, fill out the DOCX, and KEEP the sib-qa-lab project for Meeting 10.

The tests/Unit folder is pre-installed solely to support Meeting 10. Do not work on unit tests during Meeting 09.
Dependencies vendor/ and ChromeDriver are downloaded during setup. This is a local practice app only.
